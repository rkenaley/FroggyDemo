## 2. Core Engine Architecture (`froggy_engine.py`)

* **Resolution & Timing**: Renders internally at `INTERNAL_WIDTH = 640`, `INTERNAL_HEIGHT = 360`, integer-scaled 2x to `WINDOW_WIDTH = 1280`, `WINDOW_HEIGHT = 720` at `TARGET_FPS = 60`.
* **Audio (`RetroAudioEngine`)**: Uses `pygame.mixer` at `44100 Hz` stereo (43 SFX WAV samples in `assets/sfx/*.wav`) plus a 17-track multi-section MIDI soundtrack (`assets/music/*.mid`, 48–64 bars / ~91–134s per loop generated by `midi_writer.py`) covering every biome, boss, and Grid 2 Skyfrog realm.
* **Assets (`AssetManager`)**:
  * Loads autotile sets across 7 tile families (`"jungle"`, `"sanctum"`, `"ice"`, `"volcanic"`, `"underwater"`, `"tree_ascent"`, `"sky_clouds"`), mapped across all **10 room themes** (`"canopy"`, `"temple"`, `"cavern"`, `"sanctum"`, `"hound_palace"`, `"ice"`, `"volcanic"`, `"underwater"`, `"tree_ascent"`, `"sky_clouds"`), plus dedicated 640x360 pixel-art boss backdrops.
  * Loads all 8 themed background & mid-ground parallax layers.
  * Builds animation dictionaries via `build_froggy_animations(style="classic")` (supporting 3 selectable 32x32 hero styles: `"classic"`, `"azure_shinobi"` under `assets/sprites/styles/azure_shinobi/`, and `"crimson_dart"` under `assets/sprites/styles/crimson_dart/`) and `build_enemy_animations()`.
* **Room & World Management**:
  * `TileMapRoom` builds collision geometry, 4-bit autotile bitmasks (`N=1, E=2, S=4, W=8`), destructible Cracked Burrow Rocks (`'r'`), water currents (`'<'`, `'>'`), doors (`DoorPortal`), NPCs (`FriendlyNPC`), enemies, bosses, and relic pickups (`CollectiblePowerup`).
  * Grid 2 (`map_grid == 2`) supports horizontal wrap-around rooms (`horizontal_wrap: True`, used in `l2_tree_ascent` `40x71`) where exiting left/right loops seamlessly across the opposite edge, and disables wall-jumping on outer side walls while keeping Level 1's wall-jump + double-jump reset mechanic intact.
  * `WorldManager` maintains room state, cross-room portal transitions (`update_transition` / `draw_fade_overlay`), `active_shop` (`ShopMenu`), and `key_fragments_cache`.
* **Player (`FroggyPlayer`) & Input (`SNESController`)**:
  * Coyote time (`6` frames), jump buffering (`6` frames), wall-slide/wall-jump, 360-degree tongue whip (`145 px` reach) + pendulum grapple physics.
  * `SNESController` binds `W/A/S/D` & `Arrows` (D-pad), `Space/Z/K/Enter` (Jump/Accept), `X/J` (Tongue/Grapple `Y`), `R/C/U/Shift` (Special `R`: Spore Boomerang tap / 3s Muhlaser hold `MUHLASER_MAX_CHARGE = 180`), `Q/L` (Quick-Slot `L`), `Tab/I` (`SELECT` Inventory), `H` (Hints), `B` (Builder-only warp to `boss_sanctum`), `Esc` (`START` Pause).
* **Progression, Boss Rush & Persistence**:
  * `SettingsManager` persists user customization preferences (such as active `froggy_style` across `"classic"`, `"azure_shinobi"`, `"crimson_dart"`, editable in **Main Menu -> Options**) inside `froggy_settings.json`, separate from checkpoint saves.
  * `SaveManager` (`save_to_disk` / `restore_to_game`) persists player relics (including `has_abyssal_pearl`), Master Key Fragments, room, HP, destroyed Burrow Rocks, activated checkpoints, shop stock, and consumables (`heart_nectar`, `chill_draught`, `warmth_broth`, `oxygen_reed`).
  * `BossRushManager` runs both the 5-stage Boss Rush gauntlet AND **Individual Boss Time Trials** (`BOSS_TRIAL` mode accessible from the Title Screen, recording per-boss Personal Best times in `boss_rush_records.json` under `"individual_bosses"`, equipping Stomping Boots & Red Boomerang but excluding the armor-piercing Muhlaser):
    1. `boss_sanctum`: Emperor Mantis (`EmperorMantis`, 7 HP, +15% faster across all 3 stages, 6-frame redone sprite sheet)
    2. `hound_sanctum`: Dread Hound (`DreadHoundBoss`, 16 HP weighted toward later phases P2/P3, proximity-biased 50/50 Rush/Feint, `HoundGroundSpike` Howl eruptions, full-screen Phase 3 Flamethrower sweep & 5 high `'G'` grapple hooks, redone 6-frame sprite sheet)
    3. `toad_colosseum`: Giga Toad (`GigaToadBoss`, 90 HP, enlarged 96x96 ultra-jacked 31-frame Battletoad sprite overhaul, +15% boosted baseline & max speed with continuous missing-HP speed escalation, heavy fearsome stomp/shockwaves & single-hit `HURT` guard recovery)
    4. `falo_arena`: Falo Snowman (`FaloSnowmanBoss`, 84 HP + 6-Snowflake Shield Volley, `COLD_BEAM_RECHARGE`, persistent-until-touched `'h'` Heat Vents spawning randomly from 6 choice spots, redone 6-frame 64x64 sprite sheet)
    5. `shark_sanctum`: Demon Shark (`DemonSharkBoss`, 85 HP [+30%], unstompable in water, summons `AbyssalVortex` whirlpools impeding swimming, redone 6-frame 96x64 sprite sheet)
  * `SpeedrunTimer` tracks live room/boss splits across runs.

## 3. Biome Hazard & Combat Mechanics

1. **Unified Thermometer Gauge (`body_temp`, 0.0–200.0, center normal band 75.0–125.0, baseline 100.0)**:
   * Displayed as a single unified `"TEMP"` bulb thermometer across biomes with Danger Low (`<= 25.0`), Normal center (`75.0–125.0`), and Danger High (`>= 175.0`) markers.
   * **`ice` biome**: Chills `body_temp` toward `0`; frostbite damage triggers at `0.0` (after Danger Low `<= 25.0`), and Falo's `ShieldSnowflakeProjectile` hits drop `body_temp` by `54` per hit (2 hits trigger immediate hypothermia frostbite). Countered by Geothermal Hot Springs (`frost_springs`), random choice-location Geothermal Heat Vents (`'h'` in `falo_arena` — remain active until touched by Froggy, then provide `210` frames / `3.5s` of active warming up to `~126.0` before respawning elsewhere), Checkpoint Totems (`'X'`), **Pyro Broth**, or **Falo's Heart** (`Frost Guard`).
   * **`volcanic` biome**: Heats `body_temp` toward `200`; heat-stroke triggers above `175.0`. Countered by Waterfall Mist (`'c'`), **Glacial Draught**, or **Falo's Heart** (`Heat Guard` / temp reset).
2. **Underwater Oxygen & Currents**:
   * Submerged rooms (`underwater` / `'~'` water) drain `oxygen` (`0–100`) and trigger drowning damage at low O2.
   * Restored by Air Bubble Vents (`'o'`), surfacing, **Grotto Air Reed**, or eliminated permanently by the **Abyssal Pearl** (`has_abyssal_pearl`, Demon Shark drop).
   * Current tiles `'<'` and `'>'` push Froggy along aquatic corridors (`sunken_grotto`, `shark_sanctum`); numeric portals `'1'` and `'2'` link `shark_sanctum <-> shark_treasury` and `volcanic_chasm <-> volcanic_depths`.
3. **Relics & Boss Gatekeeping**:
   * **Bosses 1–4** drop the 4 **Master Key Fragments** (`mantis`, `hound`, `toad`, `falo`) needed to unseal Door `'L'` in `toad_colosseum` -> `victory_chamber`.
   * **The Muhlaser** (`'L'` inside `hound_treasury`) fires a piercing beam when `[R]` is held for 3 seconds (`180` frames), destroying Cracked Burrow Rocks (`'r'`) and dealing heavy damage to **Demon Shark** (`shark_sanctum`).
   * **Vitality Heart Container** (`'V'`) sits in `grapple_cavern`, expanding Max HP (`+1` heart; Shopkeeper's *Life Talisman* also raises Max HP).

## 4. Safe World & Asset Modification Workflow

1. **Preferred edit targets**: Edit room geometry in `levels/<zone_id>.json` (or visually via `py level_studio.py`). Avoid hardcoding level tweaks inside `froggy_engine.py`.
2. **Topology & Legend checks**: Use `world_data.validate_zone_blueprint()` or run `py map_tool.py reach` after editing doors or grids to guarantee zero broken portals or orphaned zones.
3. **Headless verification**:
   ```powershell
   py test_tools_and_modularity.py
   py map_tool.py doors
   py sprite_inspector.py --list
   ```
